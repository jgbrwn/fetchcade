# Legal and responsible use

Fetchcade is software for searching Internet Archive metadata, requesting remote files in a browser, and handing them to an emulator/player. It does not grant permission to download, possess, copy, or play any software.

Internet Archive contains material with different copyright and license statuses. Before using a result, confirm that you have a lawful basis to access and use it—for example, you created it, own the relevant rights, received permission, or the applicable license/public-domain status allows it. Follow Internet Archive's terms and all laws that apply to you.

Fetchcade does not encourage copyright infringement, does not verify the legal status of search results, and does not provide legal advice. The person operating the browser is responsible for their choices.

The optional local replay cache is controlled by the user and lives in the browser's Cache Storage. It is not a server-side ROM library. Clearing it removes local copies from that browser; it does not affect Internet Archive or any other upstream service.
